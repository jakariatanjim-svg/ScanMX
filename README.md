# ScanMX

ScanMX turns photos of paper documents into clean, straightened scans—all in your browser, on your device. No account or photo upload is needed.

## What you can do

- Find page edges automatically, correct perspective, and adjust corners by hand.
- Reduce folds, shadows, uneven lighting, and paper tint with Smart clean; choose color, grayscale, or black-and-white styles.
- Straighten curved or wavy text and rotate pages.
- Add pages from your device, camera, clipboard, or by dragging images into the app.
- Export a multi-page PDF or save an individual page as a PNG.
- Switch between English and বাংলা. Your language choice is remembered on your device.

## Open ScanMX

Open the [latest ScanMX release](https://github.com/jakariatanjim-svg/ScanMX/releases/latest) and download its web build. Open a standalone HTML download directly in a current browser. If the release provides a ZIP, extract it and open `index.html`. ScanMX is ready to use without an account or separate installation.

For camera access, open ScanMX in a browser context that allows camera use, such as a secure website or localhost. You can still choose image files if camera access is unavailable.

## Requirements

Use an up-to-date version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. Processing speed and the largest usable image size depend on your device and browser; very large photos may need more memory.

## Privacy

Your photos are processed locally in your browser. ScanMX does not upload images, use OCR, require an account, or contact a scanning service. Scans and exports stay on your device unless you choose to share them.
